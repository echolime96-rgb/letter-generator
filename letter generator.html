✉️ Term 1 CSA Writing Preparation

Writing practice: Please write a letter to your pen pal called 小花, introducing yourself and your family to her.
写作练习：请给你的笔友小花写一封信，向她介绍你自己和你的家人。
Step 1: Fill in your basic information in Chinese (填写基本信息)
我在 出生 (I was born in...)
我在 长大 (I grew up in...)
Step 2: Highlight the features to describe yourself (描述自己的外貌)
Step 3: Introduce your family tree (介绍家庭概况)
我家有 ___ 口人。 There are ___ people in my family.
我家里有 ___ 和我。 My family includes ___ and me.
Step 4: Introduce your family member (介绍其中一位家人)
我想介绍我的 ___。 I want to introduce my ___.
他/她是 ___ 人。 He/She is from ___.
他/她住在 ___。 He/She lives in ___.
